A few details

What should I wear?+

Modern Traditional Wear or Formal Evening Wear

Please do not wear brown to the event.

What is the expected timeline?+

Part 1 — Umabo: Held in the morning and early afternoon. Expect high-energy singing, dancing, and the traditional gifting ceremony.

Part 2 — Wedding Reception: Held afterwards at the designated reception venue, featuring dinner, speeches, and dancing.

May I bring a plus-one?+

Only if a plus-one is indicated on your invitation.

Is parking available?+

Yes. There is plenty of parking at the venue.

What about gifts?+

Your presence is the greatest gift. Should you wish to give something, cash gifts would be most warmly appreciated.
